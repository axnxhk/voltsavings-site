About VoltSavings

Welcome to VoltSavings, your premier destination for transparent and accurate operational cost comparisons between Electric Vehicles (EVs) and Internal Combustion Engine (ICE) vehicles.

Our mission is simple: to empower daily commuters worldwide with the financial data they need to make the switch to clean, green electric mobility. We noticed a lack of unbiased, easy-to-use tools that factor in real-world metrics like local electricity tariffs and routine maintenance.

VoltSavings operates entirely in your browser. We prioritize your privacy above all else—we do not collect personal data, track your location, or require you to create an account. All calculations are strictly client-side.

Whether you are considering an electric motorcycle or a family EV, VoltSavings is here to help you forecast your long-term return on investment.
