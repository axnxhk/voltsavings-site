OUR MISSION

About VoltSavings

VoltSavings was created to solve a straightforward problem: vehicle buyers are bombarded with complex marketing claims, yet lack an honest, straightforward, and privacy-preserving way to calculate what an electric vehicle will actually save them in real daily life.

Why an Open, Client-Side Tool?

Most online calculators force users to create an account, verify an email address, and consent to marketing newsletters just to see a basic arithmetic calculation. We chose the opposite route:

  • Zero Signup: No usernames, emails, or personal tracking.
  • Local Execution: Every calculation runs strictly within your browser's local memory.
  • Universal Adaptation: Full support for global currencies, metric (km), and imperial (mile) road standards.

Our Calculation Methodology

Our formulas compare the operational cost of internal combustion engines against electric drivetrains using direct mathematical conversion based on user-supplied tariffs:

Monthly Fuel Cost = (Monthly Distance ÷ Fuel Economy) × Fuel Unit Price

Monthly EV Cost = (Monthly Distance ÷ EV Efficiency) × Electricity Tariff

Net Annual Savings = Fuel Annual Cost - EV Annual Cost
