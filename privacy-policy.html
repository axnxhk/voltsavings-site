Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Overview

VoltSavings ("we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy rights of all visitors. This Privacy Policy details the policies governing the handling of information on our website.

2. Zero Collection of Personal Identification Information

We do not require user registration. We do not store, harvest, or transmit your commute distances, fuel expenditures, or vehicle statistics. All user inputs remain within the client-side session of your device.

3. Server Logs & Network Analytics

Like virtually all web properties, standard anonymous server log data (such as browser user-agent, IP address for DDOS mitigation, and referral headers) is processed by our global hosting network infrastructure (Netlify) strictly for security and uptime maintenance.

4. Third-Party Services & Cookies

We do not operate first-party advertising tracking cookies. Should third-party tools such as Google Search Console or Google Analytics be referenced, their usage complies strictly with standard privacy frameworks and do not link to personal identifying identity.

5. Updates to This Policy

We reserve the right to amend this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological or legal updates.
