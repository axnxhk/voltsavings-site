Privacy Policy

At VoltSavings, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by VoltSavings and how we use it.

1. Information Collection

Our calculator functions entirely on the client-side (in your browser). We do not require account registration, and we do not store or collect the personal input data (distance, prices, usage days) you enter into the calculator on any database.

2. Google AdSense & Cookies

We use third-party advertising companies to serve ads when you visit our website. Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings.

3. Log Files

VoltSavings follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, and referring/exit pages. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.
