Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about EV operational economics.

What does EV Efficiency (km/kWh) mean?

In combustion engines, efficiency is measured in distance per unit of liquid fuel (km per liter). In electric vehicles, energy is measured in kilowatt-hours (kWh), equivalent to 1 unit of electricity on your home meter. A rating of 30 km/kWh means the vehicle travels 30 kilometers using 1 unit of electric energy.

What typical efficiency numbers should I enter?

Electric Bikes / Scooters: Typically deliver 25 to 45 km per kWh (or 15 to 28 mi/kWh).

Electric Sedans / Compact Cars: Typically achieve 5.5 to 7.5 km per kWh (or 3.4 to 4.6 mi/kWh).

Petrol 70cc-125cc Bikes: 40 to 55 km/L.

Petrol 1000cc-1500cc Cars: 11 to 16 km/L.

Does this calculator account for maintenance & engine oil?

This tool strictly computes direct fuel vs electric energy expenses. In reality, your savings with an EV are even higher because EVs have no engine oil, spark plugs, timing belts, or clutch assemblies, reducing routine service costs by an estimated 40-50%.

Is user data logged or sent to servers?

No. VoltSavings is built as an open, client-side calculator. Everything runs locally in your device's browser memory. We never ask for your email, login, or personal driving history.
